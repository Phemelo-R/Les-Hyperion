About Les Hyperion

Why this site exists

Les Hyperion is a personal scholarly portfolio and learning resource created to present reproducible analyses I’ve worked on throughout my life. It collects notebooks, rendered reports and short tutorials that span GIS, quantitative ecology and biostatistics. The aim is to keep work discoverable, reusable and easy to explore — whether for students, collaborators, or future-me.

What the name means

"Les Hyperion" is a name that blends the French article "Les" (the) with "Hyperion", a Titan in Greek mythology associated with observation and light — a symbolic nod to exploring and shedding light on ecological patterns using data and code. The name reflects my interest in observation-driven science: measuring, modeling and illuminating natural processes.

About the author

Hi — I am Phemelo Rutlokoane, also known as Phemelo-R going forth. I use R and Python to answer questions about ecology and public health, focusing on careful statistical modeling, transparent workflows and reproducible code. I aim to grow as a scientist and a communicator: publishing analyses that are robust, understandable and useful to others. On this site you’ll find rendered notebooks, documentation and examples designed both for demonstration and for reuse.

What you’ll find here

  • R and Python analyses spanning biostatistics, population and community ecology, and geospatial workflows.
  • Rendered notebooks and reports (HTML) you can read, share and cite.
  • Occasional tutorials and walk-throughs aimed at students and collaborators.

Updates and contributions

I update the site periodically as I finish new analyses — check the file timestamps in the repository for recency. If you have suggestions, find bugs, or want to collaborate, reach out via GitHub (open an issue or a PR) and I’ll respond as soon as I can.
