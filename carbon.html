Air travel

CO₂ per passenger = estimated aircraft fuel × 3.16 × passenger cargo allocation ÷ (economy-equivalent seats × load factor), multiplied by the selected cabin’s automatic seat-space factor. Distance is great-circle distance plus 50 km below 550 km, 100 km through 5,500 km, and 125 km above that. Fuel is interpolated between the published distance bands; extrapolation is identified.

Existing Sub-Saharan Africa allocation factors and supplied fuel curves are retained for continuity. Added regions and aircraft use ICAO v13.1. Domestic routes outside Sub-Saharan Africa use regional averages. This is a project estimate, not the official ICAO calculator. Non-CO₂ effects are excluded.

Seating fills automatically from published airline layouts where available. Economy-equivalent seats sum each cabin’s seats multiplied by its weight. The selected cabin uses the same weight. This allocates, rather than adds to, the estimated passenger emissions.

Class weights use DESNZ 2025, Table 39: regional Economy 1 / Business 1.5; wide-body Economy 1 / Premium Economy 1.6 / Business 2.9 / First 4. Applying these average weights by aircraft layout is a project convention, not an airline-specific measurement. Where no verified layout is available, the retained aircraft-type seat-equivalent estimate is labelled explicitly.

Airport names and coordinates come from OurAirports. The operator catalogue is a dated reference, not an exhaustive or live fleet register. Select the aircraft actually flown; this catalogue does not infer flight schedules. Unlisted aircraft still require a documented estimate.

Electric motorcycles

Boda-boda baseline = distance × (2.075 ÷ 45.5 × 1.28) × replacement share. Estimated electricity = distance × 0.0405 kWh/km; measured electricity takes precedence. Charging emissions = electricity × grid share × grid factor. Avoided emissions = baseline − charging emissions.

The defaults follow the supplied workbook’s corrected chart model. Kenya grid factor 0.226 kg/kWh is a retained project assumption: the workbook attributes its underlying source to Thabani, which still needs confirmation. The chart uses a rounded 28% upstream petrol uplift; the detailed sheet’s 0.58094 kg/litre factor gives a marginally different baseline.

Project charging defaults to grid in April–August 2025, then solar from September 2025. Solar has zero operational generation emissions within this boundary; the manufacture of motorcycles, batteries and solar equipment is excluded. Avoided emissions are reported separately from flight emissions and are not offsets.

Landscapes follow Wild Impact’s published list, plus Head Office — Sandton. The financial year starts on 1 July. Excel is for reporting; the JSON backup preserves records for importing.
