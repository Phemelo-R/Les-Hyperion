Method & sources

FactorValueUnitSource
Jet fuel combustion3.16kg CO₂/kg fuelICAO ICEC Methodology v9, §3
Aircraft trip fuelinterpolatedkgICAO v9 Appendix C — linear between the two bracketing stage lengths
Detour correction+50 / +100 / +125kmICAO v9 §4.2 — banded by GCD (<550 / 550–5 500 / >5 500 km)
Passenger load factorby route group—ICAO v9 Appendix A — selected from the destination region
Passenger-to-cargo factorby route group—ICAO v9 Appendix A — deducts the belly-freight share
Y-seatsper typeseatsOperator fleet records; typical all-economy configuration where not supplied
Nautical mile1.852km/nmInternational standard

This tool reports CO₂ only, not CO₂e. The 3.16 factor is combustion carbon dioxide; the calculation excludes non-CO₂ high-altitude forcing (NOₓ, contrails, water vapour). Trip fuel is read from the ICAO Appendix C table by linear interpolation between the bracketing stage lengths, at great-circle distance plus the banded detour correction. Aircraft selection is filtered to types currently in service.
